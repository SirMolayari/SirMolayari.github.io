Traditional Artistry

Hand-Crafted Animation Styles

A dedicated artistic discipline entirely separate from my 3D game development pipeline. Exploring classical, frame-by-frame physical and tactile animation mediums.

ℹ️ Note: The techniques below represent offline, physical and traditional fine-art animation methods, practiced distinctly from real-time digital 3D game engines.
🏺
TACTILE CLAY / ARMATURE

Stop-Motion Claymation

Frame-by-frame physical manipulation of sculpted clay figures over jointed armatures under physical studio lighting, capturing organic warmth, tangible volume, and classical artisan storytelling.

✂️
LAYERED PAPER / COLLAGE

Cut-out Animation

Multi-layered paper, cardboard, and graphic cut-out puppetry on a glass multiplane setup, blending stylized graphic design with articulated jointed movements.

👤
SHADOW PUPPETRY / HIGH CONTRAST

Silhouette Animation

Stark, expressive shadow puppetry filmed with backlit screens, relying on pure silhouette shapes, delicate filigree profiles, and cinematic high-contrast visual poetry.

🧍
LIVE-ACTION STOP-MOTION

Pixilation

An avant-garde stop-motion technique utilizing live human actors and real-world physical objects as frame-by-frame animated subjects to produce surreal, physics-defying motion.
